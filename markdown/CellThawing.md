# Protocol - Cell Thawing
2022.08.06

## Protocol
### Preparation:
1. Find the location of the desired cell line within [the Dropbox file](https://www.dropbox.com/home/Chen%20Lab_inventory/Inventory?preview=Cell+lines+in+Chen+Lab+liquid+nitrogen+tank+and+-150+freezer.xlsx); write the coordinates onto a paper and take the paper.
2. Take one beaker from the holder on the wall above the sink in the cell lab.
3. Take half a beaker of water from the tap and microwave it for ~30s.
4. Use a thermometer to measure the water temperature, which ideally should be higher than 37 °C
5. Put the beaker in the dry bath with metal granules.
6. Put back the thermometer.
7. Set up the hood.
8. Take the E8 medium and Y27632 (ROCKi) from the 4°C freezer, sterilize them with alcohol wipes from cap/lid to body and put them in the hood.
	- @ ROCKi was used to prevent cell death. Drastic cell death would occur 12-16 hr after passaging or thawing. Therefore, ROCKi needs to exist for at least this long.
9. Take the coated plate out of the freezer of incubator (do not let the plate come in contact with any surface) and put it in the hood (==no alcohol wiping!==)
10. Aliquot (pour) 50 mL of E8 medium from the bottle to a 50 mL centri tube and label the tube properly (illustration below); aliquot 5 mL of E8 medium from the 50 mL centri tube to a 15 mL centri tube.
	![[L25-1 Label on the tube of aliquoted 50mL medium.png|400]]

### Fetching Frozen Cells:
1. Take the foam box from the workbench room, take the tweezer from the bench, and take the knitted gloves from the box left to the “amber” -80°C freezer.
2. Head to the cell storage room with the lab coat on.
3. Open the foam box and place it near the tank and the ladder so that I could directly take the rack out of the tank and place it in the box.
4. Wear the big PPE gloves, stable and stand on the ladder, open the tank and press the button under the panel to get rid of the fog formed by liquefied water vapor due to the low temperature.
5. Spin racks inside the tank (with PPE gloves on and keep pressing the button) to find the desired one and pull the rack out to the foam box. The rack is pretty heavy, please be prepared.
6. Close the tank.
7. Wait for the liquid nitrogen to go away and flow into the foam box (This might take minutes).
8. Take off the big PPE gloves and wear the knitted gloves; use the tweezer to open the bar/bolt on the rack and locate the box according to the coordinates acquired from the documents.
9. Take out the box carefully by gently pushing it from behind; be careful of liquid nitrogen remaining on or in the box especially when the box is in a lower position.
10. Use the tweezer to take one cryovial of the desired type of cells and put it in the foam box.
11. Put the box back in its place and re-bolt the rack with the bar.
12. Wear the big PPE gloves, stable and stand on the ladder, open the tank and put the rack back in its place slowly; make sure the handle of the rack is in a flat position and then close the tank.
13. Make sure the tank is closed (double-check).
14. Tilt the foam box, if necessary, to ensure that the vial of frozen cells is completely soaked in liquid nitrogen.
15. Take the knitted gloves, the tweezer and the box with cells inside back to the cell lab. Do not spill the liquid nitrogen!

### Thawing
1. Use the tweezer to pick the cryovial of cells in the prepared warm water bath and swirl it in the water quickly; some liquid nitrogen might still be residual on the vial, be careful.
2. Use hands to swirl the vial in the water for some more time until the substance in the vial was completed thawed.
3. Sterilize the vial with alcohol wipes and put it in the hood.

### Plating
1. Pipette cells from the cryovial to the prepared 15 mL centri tube with 5 mL medium inside ==dropwise==.
2. Invert the tube 2~3 times to ensure a homogenous mixture and dilution of DMSO.
3. Centrifuge the tube for 5 min with 300 g (check these configurations before starting; remember to balance).
4. (During centrifugation) Remove the medium from the well and put 1 mL fresh medium in the well
5. (During centrifugation) Add 1 μL Y27632 stock solution (10 mM) to the fresh medium in the well
	- For some stock solutions of chemicals and factors with low dosage and high concentration, flick the tube or centrifuge it before adding for convenience.
6. After the centrifugation is finished and the spin speed is back to 0 (sound notification by the centrifuge), take out the tube carefully.
7. Sterilize the tube with alcohol wipes carefully and steadily (if there is an accident, re-centrifuge them) and put it in the hood.
8. Use a 10 mL serological pipette to carefully remove most of the supernatant and remove the rest 1 mL with pipette tips (a small amount of the medium can be left in the tube to ensure the preservation of cells because the pellet is usually not visible for most PSCs).
9. Add 1 mL fresh medium and resuspend the cells by pipetting up and down 2-3 times.
10. Move the cell suspension to the well with pre-added 1 mL medium + Y27632
11. Shake the plate in a “+” manner to ensure a homogeneous mixture.
12. Frame and mark the well(s) with a marker to distinguish it/them from others:![[L25-2 Label on the plate's lid.png|500]]
13. Take the plate out of the hood and inspect the cells under a microscope (turn on the microscope by pressing the “auto on/off” button on the edge of the object platform; do not touch the button under the tape).
14. Put the plate into the incubator.
15. Seal the caps/lids and store the remaining bottled E8 medium, aliquoted medium in the 50 mL tube and Y27632 in the 4°C freezer in the cell lab room.
16. Clean the hood, turn off the pump valve, close the window sash and turn on the UV light; turn off the microscope and the centrifuge.
17. Put the knitted gloves, the foam box and the tweezer back in their places. if there is remaining liquid nitrogen in the box, just wait for it to be vaporized and then return the box.
18. Pour the water from the beaker into the sink and put the beaker back in the holder on the wall.
19. Re-check the hood and other instruments to ensure everything was in its proper shape..
